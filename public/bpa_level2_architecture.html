Level 2 Sub-Process Decomposition

Group 1.0

Workforce & Position Management

  • 1.1 Department Setup Define org hierarchies, units, and manager linkages.
  • 1.2 Job Position Specification Standardize titles, requirements & salary ranges.
  • 1.3 Requisition Publishing Transform position templates into public job postings.
Group 2.0

Candidate Sourcing & Portal

  • 2.1 Public Job Discovery Public career listing search & general contact forms.
  • 2.2 Profile & Portfolio Setup Register candidate credentials, skills & document uploads.
  • 2.3 Application Submission Formal submission with reference code tracking.
Group 3.0

Screening & Interview Management

  • 3.1 Application Screening Review candidate portfolio & update status via Kanban.
  • 3.2 Interview Scheduling Organize dates, invite interviewers, send candidate mail.
  • 3.3 Structured Assessment Score technical, communication, and culture dimensions.
Group 4.0

AI Decision Support & Analytics

  • 4.1 Candidate AI Scoring OpenRouter LLM candidate match score (0-100%) & gap analysis.
  • 4.2 Pipeline Bottleneck Insights Macro pipeline analysis & action recommendations.
  • 4.3 Metric Analytics & Reports Time-to-hire, funnel conversion, and summary reports.
Group 5.0

Offer Management & Contracting

  • 5.1 Offer Letter Drafting Structure salary, employment terms, start date & benefits.
  • 5.2 Offer Transmission & Decision Dispatch offer letter email, track acceptance or decline.
Group 6.0

Onboarding & Transition

  • 6.1 Onboarding Initiation Assign officer, set orientation & training dates.
  • 6.2 Checklist & Verification Track task completion & verify compliance documents.
  • 6.3 Employee Provisioning Convert Applicant role to Employee & provision portal.
